Research

Research themes

My work combines low-dimensional magnetic materials and heterostructure engineering with electrical characterization and magnetic dynamics. I explore how symmetry, interfaces, geometry and magnetic configurations can be used to develop new spintronic functionalities.

01 / Spin–orbit physics & spin transport

Charge–spin conversion, pure-spin-current transport and dynamics, magnetotransport, and symmetry-dependent spin polarizations in low-symmetry quantum materials.

Spin–orbit torqueSpin conversionTransport

02 / Engineered vdW magnetism

Magnetic heterostructures, nanopatterned structures, interfaces, interlayer interactions and non-trivial magnetic configurations in layered magnets.

2D magnetsInterfacesAltermagnetism

03 / Magnetic dynamics & functionality

Electrical control and readout of magnetic states, multidirectional torques, collective spin dynamics and emerging magnetic / spin textures.

ST-FMRDynamicsSpin textures

04 / Spin-based logic & unconventional computing

Electrical readout of complex magnetic configurations, magnonic logic and physical computing approaches based on programmable spin systems.

Spin logicMagnonicsPhysical computing

Research vision

Establish programmable magnetism and spin dynamics in engineered van der Waals systems, connecting control of magnetic configurations and interlayer interactions to collective excitations and unconventional information processing.

Biography

About

Dr. Bing Zhao is an experimental physicist specializing in charge–spin interconversion, spin-dependent transport, spin–orbit torque, van der Waals magnetism and dynamics. His recent leading-author works address field-free magnetization switching in vdW magnets, multidirectional spin–orbit torque and electrical detection of magnetic spin textures.

Nov. 2024 – Present

Staff Scientist · Chalmers University of Technology

Leads experimental projects on vdW-magnet spintronics from concept to publication; responsible for ST (VNA) FMR and cryogenic transport setups; supervises PhD and master's students and coordinates an EIC AMSwitch collaboration on 2D altermagnets.

Nov. 2023 – Oct. 2024

Researcher · Chalmers University of Technology

Led field-free spin–orbit torque work on Fe₅GeTe₂-family magnets; developed device fabrication and measurement workflows and co-coordinated external 2DSOTECH collaboration.

Nov. 2020 – Oct. 2023

Postdoctoral Researcher · Chalmers University of Technology

Developed van der Waals heterostructures for spin–charge conversion devices and measurements.

Jan. 2020

Doctor of Engineering

University of Science and Technology Beijing, China · Supervisors: Prof. Yong Jiang and Prof. Saroj P. Dash.
